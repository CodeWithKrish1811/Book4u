How Campus Book Exchange Works

Three straightforward steps to share course books with peers

1

Discover & Wishlist

Search by title, author, course code, or syllabus subject. Save books to your wishlist to track availability.

2

Connect With Owner

Request an exchange, loan, or giveaway directly with the registered campus student who owns the volume.

3

Meet & Exchange

Hand over textbooks in person at the campus library, cafeteria, or department common rooms with zero retail markups.
