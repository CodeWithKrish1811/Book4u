How Book4u Works

Simple, safe, and transparent textbook exchange in 4 straightforward steps.

1

Register as a Student

Create your campus profile using your university credentials to verify your peer identity within our student community.

2

List or Discover a Book

Post textbooks you've completed or search required semester titles by course code, faculty subject, author, or ISBN.

3

Send or Receive a Request

Connect directly with the book owner to coordinate swap preferences, free gift pickup, or agreed loan duration.

4

Meet on Campus Handover

Meet safely at the campus central library, student union, or department lobby to inspect and complete the handover.

Campus Handover Guidelines

To ensure safe and reliable peer exchanges, always follow our recommended student handover checklist:

  • 🏛️ Designated Meeting Stations: Meet in public campus spots such as the Central Library Block B or the Student Center cafeteria.
  • 🔍 Inspect Book Condition: Verify edition, page completeness, and notes before finalizing your swap or temporary loan.
  • 📅 Honor Loan Durations: Return borrowed textbooks promptly on or before the agreed semester return date.
